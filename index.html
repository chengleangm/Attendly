About Attendly

Simple attendance management for small and medium businesses.

Attendance inside Telegram

Attendly is a Telegram-based attendance system. Your staff do not need to install a new app. They can use Telegram to check in, check out, request leave, and share GPS location.

Admins can approve staff, view daily reports, manage leave requests, and export monthly Excel reports for company records.
